# Chromeflesh: Invasion 🤖📺☠️

A **Chromeflesh** add-on for NeoForge 1.21.1. Needs [Chromeflesh](https://github.com/jdj212223-ctrl/chromeflesh) 0.8+.

## What happens
- 📺 **Old TV**: right-click to turn it on (static). On **day 20** every TV that's on switches to an **emergency alert**: a rogue r̷o̴b̴o̸t̶ has breached containment. Take shelter, or RUN.
- 🤖 **The breach**: the first android, **UNIT-0**, appears near a player. Every night after that, more androids come (2 the first night, one more each night, up to 12 per player).
- 🦠 **Infection**: every android hit infects you 25%. At 100% you're **corrupted**: you lose control (you just crouch there, glitching, with an OVERRIDE IN PROGRESS screen), your name glitches, and **anyone who stays near you gets infected too**. Androids ignore the corrupted: you're on their side now.
- 🌑 Androids corrupt the grass and dirt they walk on.

## How to survive
- 💉 **Purge Shot** (empty injector + microchip + glistering melon → 2 shots): right-click a corrupted player to bring them back, or use it on yourself while you still can.
- 🛡️ A Chromeflesh **Firewall** implant keeps the virus out completely.
- ⏳ Nobody is stuck forever: a corrupted player's own system fights back after 15 minutes.

## Config (`serverconfig/chromeflesh_invasion-server.toml`)
`invasion`, `invasionDay` (20), `infection`, `corruptedMinutes` (15), `maxAndroids` (12), `griefing`.

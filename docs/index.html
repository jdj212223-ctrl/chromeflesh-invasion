What's inside

📺

The TV

Turn on an old TV. On day 20 it plays the emergency alert.

🤖

The breach

UNIT-0 appears. Every night after, more androids come (up to 12).

🦠

Infection

Every android hit infects you 25%. At 100% you're corrupted.

👾

Corrupted

You lose control, your name glitches, and you infect everyone near you.

💉

Purge Shot

Cures a corrupted player, or yourself while you still can.

🛡️

Firewall

A Chromeflesh Firewall implant keeps the virus out completely.

🌑

Corrupted ground

Androids corrupt the grass and dirt they walk on.

🎵

Override

An original invasion theme plays at the breach and every invasion night.

Get started

Install Chromeflesh 0.8+ and this add-on.
Build an Old TV and turn it on.
Survive until day 20...
...then take shelter. Or RUN. 🏃
